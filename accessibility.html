Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2026.

Commitment

I want this portfolio to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards only, zoom, or high-contrast settings. This statement describes the standard I aim for and what to do if you run into a barrier.

Target standard

This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. The Israeli web accessibility standard (ת"י 5568) is based on WCAG 2.0 AA, and this site is built to satisfy the same core requirements.

What has been done

  • Clear page structure with headings, landmarks (header, navigation, main, footer), and a "skip to main content" link.
  • Text and background colours meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.
  • Every image has a text description that explains what it shows.
  • All functions work with a keyboard, and focus is visible.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • The page does not use flashing content, autoplaying media, or motion effects.
  • Links that open a new tab are announced as such.
  • The language of the page is declared.

Known limitations

  • The music videos are hosted on YouTube. Their accessibility depends on YouTube's player; I can add captions or lyrics text on request.
  • Some project images are screenshots or video stills, and their descriptions are summaries rather than full descriptions of every detail.

Report a barrier

If you cannot access any part of this site, please use the contact form (opens in a new tab) and describe the page and the problem. I will reply and aim to fix the issue within 14 days, or tell you when it will be fixed.

Review

This statement is reviewed whenever the website changes significantly, and at least once a year.
