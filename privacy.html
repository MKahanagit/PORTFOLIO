Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Who operates this website

This portfolio is operated by Michal Kahana. For any privacy question, use the contact form on the portfolio website.

What data this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising, or tracking scripts, and it does not collect personal information through forms or accounts.

Your browser and the hosting provider may record standard technical data (such as IP address and the pages requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure. The hosting provider processes this data under its own policy.

If you use the contact form, the details you enter (name, email address, message, and your consent) are submitted through Google Forms and delivered to me. I use them only to respond to you. I delete them when the conversation has ended, and I do not share them with anyone.

Google Forms processes the submitted data under Google's own privacy policy. Please review it before submitting.

If you contact me on LinkedIn, LinkedIn's privacy policy applies to that exchange.

Embedded and linked content

Videos are hosted on YouTube. Project links open external sites hosted on GitHub (GitHub Pages) and links to YouTube and LinkedIn open those services. Each has its own privacy policy and may collect data when you use it.

Images

Photographs of people on this site are included with the consent of the people shown. If you appear in an image and want it removed, please contact me.

Changes

If this website starts collecting personal data (for example through a contact form or analytics), this policy will be updated before that change goes live.
